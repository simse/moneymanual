Interest earned on both your original deposit and on the interest already added to your account. The reason long-term savings grow so quickly.

<Callout label="Worked example">
£1,000 saved at 5% paid yearly grows to £1,629 after 10 years. The first year earns £50; by year 10 you're earning £77 — because you're also earning interest on the interest.
</Callout>
